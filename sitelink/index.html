How a job works

  1. Hire

    Search verified workers by trade, area and rating, then post your job.

  2. Attend

    The worker checks in and out. Late arrivals are recorded.

  3. Work

    A checklist built from your job post shows progress task by task.

  4. Verify

    You approve the work or ask for a fix before the job closes.

  5. Review

    Both sides leave feedback, and it updates the worker's reliability score.
